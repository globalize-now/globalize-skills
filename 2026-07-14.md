Plus `dist/compiler/per-locale-build.js` (internal `__PARAGLIDE_STATIC_LOCALE__` contract).

- **`compiler-options.d.ts`**: doc-comment-only edit (mentions stale LSP JSDoc caching as another reason to disable a TS option). No new compiler option.

### What a stamp is, and why no existing level could see one

A **stamp** is prose asserting a measurement was taken *at* a version: *"verified against the linter's rule directory at `v0.10.4`"*, *"Measured on `@lingui/cli@6.8.0`"*. Levels 1–3 watch manifest specs, declared ranges and resolutions; category 4 watches install lines; category 5 watches pinned CDN URLs. **None of them reads prose.** Both Herb stamps went stale for 18 days, the three `web-ext` stamps for 22, and in both cases a human found them by hand, late, while reading something else.

### The two discriminators, because the plain grep returns mostly noise

**1. A stamp is not a floor.** *"backward-compatible **since** `@swc/core` 1.15.0"* and *"`@vitejs/plugin-react@6.0.0` **dropped** the `babel` option"* name the version at which something **changed**. They are permanently true and never age; re-stamping one would be a regression. The first cut reported **41** "stale stamps" and most were floors. Today's classification of the 90 attributed mentions:

| class | count | ages? |
|---|---|---|
| **STAMP** | **11** | **yes — the finding** |
| FLOOR | 69 | no |
| BOTH | 1 | ambiguous, printed |
| OTHER | 9 | unclassifiable, printed |

**The BOTH and OTHER buckets are counted and printed rather than dropped, so the recall gap is visible instead of being reported as clean.**

**2. Nearest-name attribution is wrong, and wrong silently.** On `rails.convert.md:91` — *"… is the intended replacement for `erb_lint`. **As of `v0.10.4` (2026-09-10)**, Herb's rule set covers …"* — `erb_lint` ends **10** characters before the version and `Herb` starts **16** after it. Nearest-token hands the stamp to `erb_lint`, whose `latest` is `0.9.0`, concludes `0.10.4` is **newer** than latest, and drops the row. **A false negative on one of the two stamps item 87 was written about.**

The fix is a measurement, not a grammar rule: **a stamp of `0.10.4` is a stamp of whichever package published `0.10.4`.**

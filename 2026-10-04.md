### A claim checked on the way past, and it holds

The same references assert that plugin-react v6 "dropped the `babel` option, making the Babel-based Lingui macro a silent no-op". The claim is **correct** and is left alone. (6.x runs Babel plugins through the separate `@rolldown/plugin-babel` optional peer and a `reactCompilerPreset` export, which is a different shape, not the same option.)

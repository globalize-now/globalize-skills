## Mover 2 — `intl-messageformat` 11.2.14

`package.json` diff is two lines: `version`, and `@formatjs/icu-messageformat-parser` 3.5.16 → 3.5.17. No `engines` (still absent), no peers, `@formatjs/fast-memoize` stays 3.1.7.

Three files differ in the tarball: `index.d.ts`, `index.js.map`, `intl-messageformat.iife.js`.

**The `.d.ts` change is an emit-style change with no surface change.** Declarations that were previously bare (`interface Formats`, `declare class IntlMessageFormat`, …) and re-exported at the bottom via a trailing `export { … }` list are now marked `export` inline, and the trailing block collapses to `export { IntlMessageFormat as default }`. Diffing the union of declared and exported names between the two versions: **identical, 34 names.** Nothing was added, removed, or renamed.

**The bundle change is purely cosmetic.** Both files are 2806 lines. The complete diff is four hunks, all formatting:

- a dropped `/* @__PURE__ */` annotation on `IDENTIFIER_PREFIX_RE`;
- braces added around two `if`/`else` bodies that were previously brace-less but already unambiguous (in `parseMessage`'s tag handling and in the quoted-literal loop);
- a `default:` case collapsed onto one line, dropping a trailing `break` that was **the last statement of the last case** — a no-op.

No control flow, no identifier, and no literal changed. This reads as a Prettier/rollup config bump in the FormatJS repo, not a behavior change.

`^11` admits it, `intl-messageformat` is a tracked transitive rather than a direct install line, and no skill asserts on parser internals. **Not actionable** — same disposition as every other 11.2.x patch, and it folds into open item 7.

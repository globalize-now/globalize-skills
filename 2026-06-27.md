1. **`@lingui/swc-plugin` 6.4.0 → 6.5.0** (2026-06-26) — minor, **additive**. swc-plugin is versioned independently in its own repo (`lingui/swc-plugin`), which is why it is ahead of the rest of the `@lingui` v6 family (still 6.4.0). The skills' `^6` pin admits it. **Action:** none required now; folded into open item #2 (mention the new options when the Lingui-v6 setup refresh happens) and item #3 (re-verify swc_core ABI through 6.5.0 once a GitHub read path exists, since the exact release notes are GitHub-Releases-only and were not reachable this run).
2. **`intl-messageformat` 11.2.8 → 11.2.9** (2026-06-26) — patch. The only change is a transitive bump of `@formatjs/icu-messageformat-parser` `3.5.11 → 3.5.12`; `@formatjs/fast-memoize` and `engines` unchanged. Within `^11`, no setup-prose impact. **Not actionable.**
